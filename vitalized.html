The project

Vitalized needed a completely new website - not a reskin of an existing template, but a ground-up redesign built around a proper design system. Every component, flow, and state was designed from scratch in Figma, structured as a living design system that the development team could build from and extend over time.

The design language draws from the brand's core values - premium, clean, science-backed - expressed through a deep forest green palette, confident typography, and a layout system that gives the products room to breathe.

The site was built on Shopware CMS, with all components designed for direct integration. Responsive across desktop, mobile and PWA breakpoints.

Component design

The product card is the most repeated element across the site. Getting it right means designing not just the default state, but every variation a real product can be in.

Default New Sale 20% off Clearance Low expiry date Out of stock Discontinued Partner - logged in Partner - logged out
every state, every context
Approach
01
Systems over screens

Every component was built to be reusable and documented with its variants and states before it appeared in a page layout. Page designs were assembled from the component library, not drawn from scratch each time.

02
Edge cases are part of the design

A product card that only works for in-stock products isn't finished. Designing for clearance, out of stock, discontinued, and expiry states means the UI holds up when real inventory data is unpredictable.

03
Responsive from the start

Desktop and mobile were designed in parallel. The Figma file contains paired frames for every major template, including a PWA view optimised for app-like behaviour on mobile devices.

Content pages

Beyond the shop, Vitalized needed a full ingredient encyclopaedia - a filterable library of every active ingredient, each with its own detail page.

The ingredient pages combine scientific content with product recommendations, linking knowledge directly to purchase. Designed to build trust and support the brand's science-backed positioning.
